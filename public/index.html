Labtrail

Labtrail reads your lab reports and shows each result next to the range printed on that report. It does not diagnose or give medical advice. Talk to a doctor about what your results mean for you.

Privacy. When you add a report, the file is sent to Claude (Anthropic) to read the numbers. Labtrail does not store your files or results on its servers; results stay in this browser only, so back them up from the Reports tab. Labtrail counts anonymous totals such as visits and reports read. Inside the Instagram app, open the link in Safari or Chrome so your data is not lost when the in-app browser closes.
